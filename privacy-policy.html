Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This application is a personal automation tool used to publish content from the Lunivra Crochet Etsy store to its own Pinterest business account.

Information We Access

The application may access Pinterest account information, boards, and Pins necessary to create and manage content on the account owned and controlled by the application owner.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to automate the creation and publication of Pins containing product images, titles, descriptions, and Etsy product links.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally included in public source code.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the owner of Lunivra Crochet through the associated Etsy store.
